## Recomendado

## Crítica para regenerar (inglés)
Jenny must match the reference exactly: slim oval face, defined jaw, light-tan skin, long straight black hair worn loose or in a low ponytail, small stud earrings. Do not show her arm through or inside the bubble: keep the free hand resting on top from the front side or on the base. The electric inflator must be plain with no label, no text and no CE mark, or leave it out of frame. The small balloons inside should look like matte latex with slight size variation. Natural daylight, light grain.
